- **Audience:** College students who want affordable meal-prep ideas that support their diet or fitness goals.

- **Test:** I can choose a diet and fitness goal, receive matching meals, verify one displayed macro or price is labeled as an estimate/sample, and see my standing rule's effect in the actual preview. After I approve and merge, the same registered Pages URL works.
